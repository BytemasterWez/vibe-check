# Product Definition & Commercial Package (Phases 4, 4B, 11, 11B)

## MVP definition (Phase 4)

**TenderPulse** — *"Every UK public tender worth your time, scored and
explained, in one daily email."*

- **Buyer persona:** (a) owner/bid lead at 5–50-person B2G suppliers (IT,
  construction, care, facilities, marketing, training); (b) bid consultants
  managing multiple clients (white-label channel).
- **Pain:** two official portals, thousands of weekly notices, keyword
  alerts that either flood or miss; every missed niche tender is missed
  revenue with a hard deadline.
- **Before:** 1–3 h/week scanning portals or paying £125+/mo for keyword
  dumps. **After:** one digest of ≤10 scored matches, each with the reason it
  matched, deadline runway, value, and the official link.
- **Does:** ingest both official sources daily; dedupe; score against the
  client's profile; explain every point; digest + CSV; dashboard; freshness
  and failure visibility. **Does not:** write bids, cover private-sector RFPs,
  auto-send outreach, or guess when data is missing.
- **Core promise:** never miss a winnable tender in your niche again — and
  see *why* each one surfaced.
- **Worth continuing if:** buyers with real tender flow in their niche reply,
  take trials, and at least one converts to paid within 30 days of outreach.

## Manual wedge (Phase 4B)

Weeks 1–2, before any more automation: run the pipeline for 3–5 hand-picked
niches, personally review each digest (15 min/day), and send it manually as a
plain email. Acceptable manual load: ≤30 min/customer/week. Charge from day
one: **£99 fixed 30-day pilot** (founder rate, credited against year 1).
Learn: which reasons buyers cite, junk-rate complaints, niches with sparse
flow (refund + exit). Automate sending only after ≥3 pilots value the output.

## Outreach package (Phase 11)

**One-pager:** headline promise + one real scored match (title, buyer, value,
deadline, reasons) + "built on both official government feeds, updated daily".

**Email 1 (evidence-led):** "Hi {name} — while checking this week's feeds I
found {real tender title} (£{value}, closes {date}) from {buyer}, which looks
like a fit for {company}. I run TenderPulse, which scores every notice on
both official portals against your niche and explains each match. Want this
week's full list for {niche}? No charge for the sample."
**Email 2 (pain-led):** free portal alerts are keyword-only and unranked;
here's what they missed in your niche last month. **Email 3 (consultant):**
white-label your clients' digests; you keep the relationship.
LinkedIn variants: same three angles, ≤80 words each.

**Objections:** "free alerts exist" → keyword ≠ scored + explained + both
portals + dedupe; "we know our buyers" → renewal/award intelligence roadmap;
"no time" → that's the product: 10 lines/day instead of two portals.

**Qualification checklist:** sells to public sector already? ≥5 relevant
notices/month in their niche (check before pitching — sparse niches churn)?
Named person who owns bidding? Discovery questions: How do you find tenders
today? What did you last bid, and how did you find it? What have you missed?
What do you pay for alerts/consultants (budget)? Who decides £59/mo (urgency
+ authority)?

## Buyer evidence plan (Phase 11B)

50-target list construction (no invented names): pull last 90 days of
**award** notices from the two verified feeds in 3 pilot niches (e.g.
digital/software CPV 72*, training 805*, facilities 909*) → the *losing*
bidder pool is invisible, but winning SMEs prove niche flow; target similar
firms via LinkedIn Sales Nav job titles: "Bid Manager", "Bids & Tenders",
"Business Development Manager" at UK SMEs in those niches, plus bid-writing
consultancies (dozens listed publicly in UK bid-writer directories).
Pain signal: careers pages mentioning frameworks/public sector; trigger:
a live tender in their niche closing in >2 weeks (send it to them).

**Validation thresholds (measurable, 30-day):** 50 contacted → reply ≥15%,
calls ≥5, pilots ≥2 paid. Digest usefulness ≥3 of 5 "would bid" flags per
pilot month. Manual load ≤30 min/customer/week. Infra ≤£20/mo.
**Invalidating responses:** "free alerts are fine" from >70% of repliers, or
pilots who don't open digests by week 2, or <5 relevant notices/month in all
three pilot niches.

## Metrics tracked from day one

Contacted / replies / calls / pilots offered / pilots paid / MRR ·
ingestion success rate & freshness (from `ingest_runs`) · quarantine % ·
digest junk-rate feedback · manual minutes per customer · infra + LLM spend.
